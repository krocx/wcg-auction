The auction rules

    Player categories

    CategoryPlayersBase price

    Base prices come straight from the player list in the auction sheet — a few Category 2 and 3 players carry a lower base than their category's standard price.

    How a player is sold

    • The auctioneer draws a player — Category 1 first, then 2, 3, 4 — chosen at random inside each category.
    • Bidding opens at the base price. Captains call out raises: +$1M up to $20M, +$2M beyond.
    • "Going once… going twice… SOLD!" — the highest bid takes the player.
    • The system blocks any bid that would leave a team unable to complete its squad.
    • Round 2: unsold players return. A team that still needs players must take them; if only one team needs a player, it gets them at base price.
